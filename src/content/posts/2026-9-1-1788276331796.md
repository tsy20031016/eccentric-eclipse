Taking tomato sauce with chicken thighs as an example:

1. Prepare the chicken thighs and tomatoes, and dice them both—cut the chicken thighs into slightly larger pieces.

2. Add two slices of ginger to the chicken thighs, along with an appropriate amount of soy sauce (both light and dark), oyster sauce, and a dash of sesame oil. Let it marinate for a while.

3. Prepare the sauce: Combine light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, chicken bouillon, MSG, and cornstarch (this is key for thickening the sauce).

4. Heat a pan over high heat with a little oil. Add the marinated chicken and stir-fry until just cooked through. Remove and set aside.

5. Add a little more oil to the pan. Add the diced tomatoes and stir-fry until they release their juices. Then add the chicken cubes and the prepared sauce. Cover the pan and simmer for a few minutes.
