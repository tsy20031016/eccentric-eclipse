1.升维度用隐函数求雅可比矩阵

考虑 n维球坐标换元的雅可比矩阵：

\[
\begin{cases}
x_1=r\cos\theta_1,\\
x_2=r\sin\theta_1\cos\theta_2,\\
x_3=r\sin\theta_1\sin\theta_2\cos\theta_3,\\
\quad\vdots\\
x_{n-1}
=r\sin\theta_1\sin\theta_2\cdots
\sin\theta_{n-2}\cos\theta_{n-1},\\
x_n
=r\sin\theta_1\sin\theta_2\cdots
\sin\theta_{n-2}\sin\theta_{n-1},
\end{cases}
\]

\[
r\geq0,\qquad
0\leq\theta_1,\theta_2,\ldots,\theta_{n-2}\leq\pi,
\qquad
0\leq\theta_{n-1}<2\pi.
\]

\[
\boxed{
dx_1dx_2\cdots dx_n
=
r^{n-1}
\sin^{n-2}\theta_1
\sin^{n-3}\theta_2
\cdots
\sin\theta_{n-2}
\,dr\,d\theta_1d\theta_2\cdots d\theta_{n-1}
}
\]
